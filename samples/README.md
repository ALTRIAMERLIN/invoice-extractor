# Public demo invoices

These three invoices are fictional and generated for this project. Names, addresses, invoice identifiers and amounts are synthetic. They may be used and shared under this repository's MIT license. They are not valid for payment or accounting.

The PDFs illustrate three supported English label layouts, including a two-page invoice and explicit currencies. `expected.json` records their independently specified fields and source pages.

In the app, select **Load demo invoices** to try them without uploading personal documents. These samples illustrate supported behavior; they do not establish accuracy on arbitrary supplier PDFs.
